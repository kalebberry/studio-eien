### The Calm Paradigm

Mainstream personal finance applications operate under an adversarial visual paradigm. Flashing red metrics, aggressive trend lines, and persistent push notifications turn financial awareness into an anxiety trigger. For neurodivergent individuals—especially those managing ADHD or executive dysfunction—this punitive sensory overload leads directly to avoidance and financial paralysis.

**Mika (美香)** reframes the relationship between human and ledger. By shedding aggressive gamification in favor of editorial clarity, generous whitespace, and muted earth tones, Mika transforms personal budgeting from an emotional trial into an unhurried, reassuring ritual.

### Designing for Executive Ease

Executive dysfunction thrives on ambiguity and cognitive friction. Mika eliminates decision fatigue through single-decision horizons, progressive disclosure, and a strict rule against judgmental colors. Financial health is communicated through structural balance rather than alarmist alerts.
