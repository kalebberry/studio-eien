	<p>
		Most digital finance tools are designed around high-arousal sensory stimulation. Red deficit

	<p>
		For neurodivergent individuals—especially those navigating ADHD or executive dysfunction—this
		visual clutter creates intense cognitive friction. When opening a budget app feels like facing an
		accusation, the natural human response is complete avoidance, causing financial health to suffer
		in silence.
	</p>
	<p>
		<strong>Mika (美香)</strong> was born from a desire to create a profoundly different experience:
		an unhurried, reassuring sanctuary where money is managed with quiet confidence and emotional peace.
	</p>
</EditorialSection>

<EditorialSection
	eyebrow='APPROACH // 02'
	title='Broadsheet Clarity & Low-Arousal Interaction'
	lead='Budgeting reimagined as a peaceful morning ritual. No flashing alarms, no judgmental scores, no sensory noise.'
	layout='asymmetric'
	signature='SECTION: ARCHITECTURE ⟡ METHOD SIGNATURE: V.03'
>
	<p>
		Mika discards the dashboard metaphor entirely. Drawing inspiration from traditional Japanese
		merchant ledgers (<em>Daifukuchō</em> 大福帳) and minimalist editorial broadsheets, the interface
		presents financial information with spacious, calm typography and soft, paper-like tonality.
	</p>
	<ul>
		<li>
			<strong>Zero-Judgment Visual Language:</strong> Deficits and surpluses are communicated through
			deliberate typographic measure and layout hierarchy, entirely eliminating aggressive red hues.
		</li>
		<li>
			<strong>Single-Horizon Decision Making:</strong> The interface presents only one decision at a
			time—such as reviewing weekly groceries or categorizing an unassigned transaction—preventing
			the cognitive paralysis of 50-row spreadsheets.
		</li>
		<li>
			<strong>Tactile Ledger Rhythm:</strong> Generous whitespace and muted dividers transform daily
			accounting into an orderly, reassuring ritual.
		</li>
	</ul>
	<p>
		By respecting human sensory limits, Mika turns budgeting from an intimidating chore into a
		grounding daily practice.
	</p>
</EditorialSection>
